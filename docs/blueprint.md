# **App Name**: LiftVault

## Core Features:

- Workout Session Logging: Log sets, repetitions, and weights for each exercise during a training session. The system tracks exercise types (e.g., squat, bench, deadlift) and allows custom entries.
- Program Progression Tracking: Monitor and visualize progression through custom or pre-defined powerlifting programs, automatically calculating next session targets based on previous performance.
- Bodyweight & Metric Tracking: Record and view historical data for body weight and other relevant personal metrics over time.
- Performance Graphs & History: Visualize lift progression over time with interactive graphs, showing maximum weights, volume, and estimated one-rep maximums for key exercises.
- Powerlifting Standards Comparison: Compare individual lift performance against 'Open Powerlifting' standards for age, weight class, and experience level, providing benchmarks.
- Bar & Plate Visualization: Dynamically display the physical arrangement of weight plates on a barbell for a given lift's weight, helping users set up their lifts correctly.
- AI-Powered Training Insights: Leverage a generative AI tool to provide personalized feedback and suggestions for workout adjustments or form tips based on logged training data and standards comparisons.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color scheme: Dark theme, reflecting strength and focus, providing a sense of seriousness and precision to the training environment.
- Primary color: A deep, professional blue (#264073), chosen for its association with stability and focus, offering a strong base against the dark background.
- Background color: A very dark, desaturated blue-grey (#21242C), which creates a clean, distraction-free canvas, ensuring that data and visuals are the main point of focus.
- Accent color: A vibrant greenish-cyan (#52E0A7), strategically used for highlighting progress, interactive elements, and key performance indicators to ensure they stand out vividly and dynamically.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif), chosen for its modern, robust, and slightly tech-informed character, perfect for emphasizing strong titles and metric readouts.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), selected for its exceptional readability and neutral, objective appearance, ideal for presenting logged data, program details, and general textual information.
- Employ sleek, minimalist icons with a contemporary aesthetic, precisely representing core concepts like weights, graphs, progress arrows, and any necessary anatomical references. Emphasis on clarity and immediate understanding.
- Implement a clean, modular, and grid-based layout that heavily prioritizes data presentation. Dashboard elements will be intuitively organized, ensuring that crucial progress indicators and historical lift data are prominently and accessibly displayed.
- Integrate subtle and functional animations for graph transitions and real-time data updates, enriching the user experience without causing distraction. Specific animations will also be used for dynamically assembling weight plates on the barbell.
